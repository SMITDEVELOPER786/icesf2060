Profile Summary

Dr. Ammad Zafar is an esteemed scholar holding a Ph.D. from the Department of Public Administration, University of Karachi. His research expertise spans education, health, transportation, entrepreneurship, financial management, and gender studies, with extensive publications in national and international journals.

His active participation in research conferences and advisory roles for academic journals reflects his strong commitment to academic excellence and professional development, positioning him as a significant contributor to his field.

Dr. Rabia Mumtaz

Assistant Professor of Management Sciences
Founding Director, Centre for Sustainability
Deputy Director (Postgraduate Programs)
Research Fellow, INTI International University, Malaysia (19 May 2026–Dec 2028)

Dr. Rabia Mumtaz is committed to advancing sustainability through academic leadership, research, industry engagement, and capacity building. In addition to her academic responsibilities, she provides sustainability consultancy and advisory services to local and international Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs), supporting organisations in strengthening ESG integration, sustainability capabilities, and responsible business practices. She is also actively associated with professional and industry organisations, promoting collaboration between academia, industry, and government to advance sustainable development.

Research Interests

Sustainable Business Performance | Green Human Resource Management (GHRM) | Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) | Green Innovation | Employee Green Behaviour | Organisational Sustainability | Climate Resilience | Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)

Education

  • MS in Management Sciences

Research, Academic & Professional Contributions

Her research examines how organisations, particularly Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs), can strengthen sustainable business performance through Green Human Resource Management (GHRM), ESG integration, organisational sustainability, green innovation, employee green behaviour, and climate resilience. Her work bridges management research with practical solutions that support responsible business practices and long-term organisational resilience.

Dr. Rabia has published peer-reviewed research in sustainability, Green HRM, organisational behaviour, and responsible management. She actively supervises research students while contributing to postgraduate research governance, curriculum development, and academic quality assurance. She has delivered keynote addresses and presented her research at national and international conferences, contributing to scholarly dialogue on sustainability, responsible management, and climate resilience.

As Principal Investigator of the Government of Sindh-supported project, Climate Resilience, Sustainability and Capacity Building Initiative for SMEs," Dr. Rabia Mumtaz leads a research and implementation program that helps SMEs strengthen climate resilience, sustainability capabilities, and sustainable business behaviour through stakeholder engagement, awareness, and capacity-building initiatives.
